Overview

AMERICAN SYSTEMS is an employee-owned federal government contractor supporting national priority programs through our strategic solutions in the areas of Information Technology, Engineering & Analysis, Test & Evaluation, and Training.

Responsibilities

Position Description: The Data Engineer – Jr supports the design, development, and sustainment of data pipelines, data integration, and basic data infrastructure that enable analytics and AI/ML solutions for CD&I. This position focuses on ingesting, transforming, and preparing data from multiple USMC and DoW systems for use by analysts, data scientists, and operational stakeholders.

Responsibilities:

- Assist in building and maintaining pipelines to move and transform data from source systems into analytic data stores.

- Implement basic data quality checks, validation routines, and data cleansing processes.

- Support the integration of structured and semi-structured data sources relevant to capability development and experimentation.

- Work with senior data engineers and analysts to understand data requirements and translate them into technical tasks.

- Contribute to the documentation of data flows, schemas, and data dictionaries.

- Implement basic data security and access controls in accordance with DoW/USMC policies and best practices.

- Troubleshoot data pipeline issues and support operations and maintenance activities.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications:

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Data Science, or related field.

- 0–3 years of experience in data engineering, database development, or related technical role.

- Basic experience with SQL and at least one programming/scripting language (e.g., Python).

- Familiarity with relational databases and concepts such as tables, views, indices, and joins.

- Ability to work in a team environment and follow established engineering standards and practices.

- Active TS/SCI Clearance

Desired Qualifications:

- Experience supporting DoW/USMC or other Federal customers.
