540 is seeking a Data Engineer to support a mission-critical federal health data modernization initiative. The team is bringing together data from diverse defense and federal systems to build scalable pipelines, APIs, and data products that improve how critical information is integrated, accessed, and used.

In this role, you’ll work hands-on with complex health and mission data to develop scalable, production-ready capabilities while helping establish reusable patterns for data ingestion, integration, and delivery. Your work will contribute to the technical foundation of a growing data platform ecosystem supporting multiple teams, data products, and mission areas.

Location: Remote within the continental United States. Periodic travel may be required

Citizenship & Clearance Requirement: per client requirements, candidates must be U.S. Citizens with an active DoW Secret (or higher) clearance

Education Requirement: Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical field (preferred)

540 Internal Thrive Level: Data Engineer II or III
