Job Description

About this Opportunity

We are on the lookout for talented Data Engineer I to join our Tech Foundations Vertical as part of our 2026 Tech Grad Program, on our journey to always deliver amazing experiences.

Our Tech Grad Program offers you a unique environment for you to learn and develop your skills, and to contribute to the development of our cutting-edge tech solutions. You will have some of the most talented and experienced software engineers in the industry as mentors to help you ramp up, and a support group you can go to for help and connection.

Our Engineering organization consists of over 1,500 talented engineers distributed across our major verticals. Our tech employees are divided into different teams across the following areas: Consumer Data Products, FinTech, Logistics, Pandora, Platform, Quick Commerce, and Vendor Tech. They work on a range of major topics from boosting customer acquisition and retention to creating efficient experiences for our vendors, building an excellent logistics ecosystem across the delivery lifecycle, providing financial empowerment to our stakeholders and much more.

As a member of the Tech Foundation Team, you’ll support Delivery Hero’s rapid innovation and growth. You’ll work on foundational systems that enable faster feature development, security, and reliability across our global engineering community. Every enhancement you make will contribute to our teams' ability to build, scale, and deliver quality features—ultimately impacting millions of users worldwide.

If you are an Engineering graduate looking for an opportunity to grow in the tech industry and make a difference, our Tech Graduate Program is for you! You can also visit our Early Careers @ Delivery Hero website for more information!

What you need to be Successful

To be eligible for our program, you must:

-
Have the right to live and work in Germany

-
Candidates within the EU are also eligible to apply as long as you meet the above criteria

-
Have (or be graduating from) a Bachelor’s or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Engineering, Data Science or a related field

-
If graduated, you may also have a maximum of 2 years of experience in similar job fields

-
Coding bootcamp graduates (recent/upcoming) are also eligible to apply and do not need to meet the degree requirement above; please apply if you have a minimum of 6-12 months’ internship/bootcamp experience

-
Have a good understanding of and direct experience with coding in relevant languages and technologies (see below), ideally from internships, bootcamps, or open-source projects

-
Be an analytical thinker and strong problem solver with a humble, inquisitive approach

-
Have good communication and collaboration skills

-
Be business fluent in English

-
Be excited about working in a fast-moving, agile company where we learn from our mistakes

Technical skills we look for:

-
Data

-
Python, SQL, noSQL, cloud services (AWS), data warehousing (BigQuery, Redshift)

Generally, we also expect your curiosity in learning/trying out new technologies.
